Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Habibi Clothing (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit or make a purchase on our website diegothegammer.github.io/habibi-clothing (the “Site”).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account information — name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Order & shipping details — full name, email, phone number, delivery address, and payment-related information needed to process and deliver your order.
  • Usage data — pages visited, products viewed, cart activity, and general device/browser information (stored locally or via analytics tools).
  • Communications — messages you send us through the contact form or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfil orders, and send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Respond to customer service requests and inquiries.
  • Improve the Site, products, and shopping experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations under South African law (including POPIA).

3. Legal Basis & POPIA

We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). We only collect information that is necessary for the purposes listed above, and we take reasonable steps to keep it accurate and secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Payment processors and logistics partners solely to complete your order.
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (e.g. hosting, analytics), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect our rights and customers.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The Site uses browser local storage to keep your shopping cart and login session. We may also use basic analytics cookies. You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Data Retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to fulfil orders, provide support, meet legal/tax requirements, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account data (subject to legal retention obligations).

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us via the Contact page or email us at the address provided on that page.
